🍽️ Restaurant Revenue Forecasting

Predictive analytics for strategic site selection — model, features, and results summary.

Business Challenge

Launching a new restaurant requires substantial capital and strategic planning. Poor location choice often leads to closures within 18 months. Data-driven forecasting helps reduce risk and improve decision-making for site selection.

Mission: Build a predictive model to forecast annual revenue, enabling informed site selection and minimizing risk.

Strategic Approach

With limited observations, it's critical to balance model complexity and generalization. We prioritized interpretable features and ensemble strategies.

Methodology Highlights

Algorithm: Random Forest ensemble for stable CV and leaderboard performance.
Feature Engineering: Simple, interpretable transformations to avoid overfitting.
Ensemble: Bootstrapped models averaged to reduce variance.
Target Transform: Log-transform on revenue improved stability.

Model Architecture

Ensemble averaging across multiple Random Forest models trained on different subsets improved robustness and reduced sensitivity to outliers.

Key insight: days_open (days since opening) was the strongest predictor. Treating certain anonymized variables as categorical improved results.

Key Performance Drivers

Primary: Operational duration (days open).
Encoding: One-hot for anonymized categorical transforms.
Ensemble: Averaging reduced variance.
